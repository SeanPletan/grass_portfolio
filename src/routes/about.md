I am a developer who is a new graduate of the University of Houston. My work sits at the intersection of real-time and embedded systems, traditional fullstack web development, and intentional, optimized systems development. Whether implementing an EDF scheduler in FreeRTOS, building GPU-accelerated WebGL simulations, or developing an AI intrusion detection system, I focus on performance, correctness, and resource-aware design.

I’m drawn to unique projects with clear intent. Indeed, this portfolio was an exercise in rethinking the conventional developer website: designing something that felt open and exploratory rather than purely technical. Inspired by time spent outdoors, I wanted the experience to feel expansive and light while still grounded in familiar interaction patterns.

I'm also a big supporter of open source projects, privacy, and user-centered decisions. I'm wary of proprietary projects, particularly when it comes to how user data is handled, and the preservation of a culturally-significant product. Many of these projects subscribe to the idea of surveillance capitalism, where user data is collected, commodified, and used for profit, often without users fully understanding the extent of it. Unfortunately, the decisions made by project maintainers can have far-reaching consequences, such as introducing features that compromise privacy or adding features that are in the interest of the business and not the user-base. Because I am just one person, I donate to the Electronic Frontier Foundation and Big Brother Watch when I can. 

In my free time, I enjoy working on and driving my NB Mazda Miata, playing Dungeons and Dragons with my friends, playing Kerbal Space Program, reading, playing pickleball with my friends, and hiking.
